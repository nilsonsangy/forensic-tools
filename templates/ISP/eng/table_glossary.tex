Backup & Backup copy. Storing information on media intended for data contingency. \\

Backup Site & Secondary Data Center. Secondary Data Processing Center - A "Backup Copy" environment connected as a "remote data mirroring" with the primary data center. Its purpose is to immediately operate in case of primary data center downtime. It is also known as "Hot Backup." \\
\hline
Firewall & 1- A system designed to logically protect a network. 2- Software and/or hardware that provides security by isolating a company's computer network from the rest of the Internet. A firewall also prevents outsiders from connecting to the network; anyone interested in maintaining information confidentiality should have one.  
*Source: IT Glossary at \url{http://www.eb.ufmg.br/bax/TecnoInfo/glossario/TI.htm} \\

Password & Key to access a system. \\
\hline
No-Break & Known as UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) worldwide. It is exactly what it is: an uninterrupted power supply source. Its main purpose is to protect your load (PCs, servers, peripherals, or other electronic equipment) against defects or failures in the power supply. \\
\hline
Worms & A worm, like a virus, is designed to copy itself from computer to computer, but a worm does this automatically by taking over functionalities in the computer capable of transporting files or information. A worm in your system can propagate without user intervention. One of the significant dangers of worms is their ability to replicate in large volumes, potentially consuming memory and causing a computer to stop functioning correctly. \\
\hline
SPAM & SPAM refers to unsolicited emails, usually sent to a large number of people. When the content is exclusively commercial, this type of message is also referred to as UCE (Unsolicited Commercial Email).  
Source: \url{http://www.nbso.nic.br/docs/cartilha/cartilha-06-spam.html} \\
\hline
Phishing Scam & Phishing is a technique used to obtain personal information for theft or fraud purposes through fraudulent use of emails that appear to be from legitimate sources. These seemingly authentic messages are designed to trick recipients into disclosing personal information, such as bank passwords, credit card numbers, etc.  
Source: \url{http://www.computerworld.com/securitytopics/security/story/0,10801,89096,00.html} and  
\url{http://www.microsoft.com/athome/security/email/phishing.mspx} \\
\hline
Pen drive & A pen drive is a device with flash memory and a USB connector that functions as a removable storage unit. Simply plug it into the computer's USB port to be recognized as a new storage unit ready for use.  
The pen drive memory is also known by other names: memory key, USB keychain, flash drive, flash memory, mini HD, among others. In Brazil, it is popularly known as a pen drive.  
The main advantage of this device is its compact size (approximately the size of a keychain) with the possibility of having a large storage capacity. You can transport it anywhere and plug it into any computer with a USB port. We can say that the pen drive functions as a removable mini HD.  
Source: \url{http://www.pendrivenet.com.br/} \\
\hline
Mobile Device & A mobile device is any electronic equipment or peripheral, battery-operated or not, that can be easily transported, has the ability to process, store, or transmit data, and is accessible anywhere.  
Examples of mobile devices: laptops, notebooks, personal digital assistants (PDAs), Blackberry, USB devices (Universal Serial Bus), wireless handheld devices, Compact Discs (CDs), Digital Versatile Discs (DVDs), flash drives, MP3/MP4 players, digital cameras, and camera phones. \\
